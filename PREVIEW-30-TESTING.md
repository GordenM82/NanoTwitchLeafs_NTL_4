# Preview 30 test checklist

- Main window: Save a large window on a multi-monitor desktop and verify after restart that it remains fully inside one monitor's work area.
- Monitor changes: Move NTL to the second monitor, restart it, then disconnect that monitor and verify that NTL returns to an available screen.
- Maximized state: Verify that maximized NTL fills only its current monitor and respects the taskbar.
- Child windows: Open Twitch login, Nanoleaf pairing, device-name input, trigger details and device groups; each must open fully on its owner monitor.
- Scrollbars: Drag vertical and horizontal thumbs, click the free track and use the mouse wheel.
- Themes/scaling: Repeat scrollbar and placement checks in Light and Dark mode at 100%, 125% and 150% scaling.
- Diagnostics: The log records the selected monitor work area and current DPI without personal display metadata beyond the Windows device name.
